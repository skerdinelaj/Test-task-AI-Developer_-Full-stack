# Cancellations (Article 5)

When a flight is cancelled, passengers are entitled to compensation unless:

- they were told about the cancellation **at least 14 days** before the scheduled departure; or
- they were told between **7 and 13 days** before departure and offered re-routing that lets them depart no more than two hours early and arrive less than four hours late; or
- they were told **less than 7 days** before departure and offered re-routing that lets them depart no more than one hour early and arrive less than two hours late; or
- the airline proves extraordinary circumstances.

The airline carries the burden of proving when and whether the passenger was informed.
Whether the re-routing offer met the time windows usually needs a human to check the airline's emails.
