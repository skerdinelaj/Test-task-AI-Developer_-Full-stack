# EU261 compensation amounts (Article 7)

Compensation is a fixed amount per passenger, based on the great-circle distance of the flight:

- **EUR 250** for flights of 1,500 km or less;
- **EUR 400** for all flights within the EU longer than 1,500 km, and for all other flights between 1,500 km and 3,500 km;
- **EUR 600** for all other flights over 3,500 km.

Article 7(2) allows the amount to be cut by 50% when the passenger reaches the final destination within a set time of the original schedule. For long flights over 3,500 km (not within the EU) this applies when the arrival delay is between three and four hours, giving EUR 300.
The distance is measured to the final destination using the great-circle method.
Compensation is separate from refunds, meals, hotel accommodation and other care the airline owes.
