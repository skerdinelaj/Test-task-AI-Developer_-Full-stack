# EU261 scope: which flights are covered

Regulation (EC) No 261/2004 (Article 3) covers a passenger with a confirmed reservation who checks in on time when:

- the flight **departs from an airport in the EU** (plus Iceland, Norway and Switzerland), whatever the airline; or
- the flight **departs from outside the EU and arrives in the EU**, and the operating airline is an **EU (Community) carrier**.

A flight from outside the EU to the EU on a non-EU airline is not covered. A flight that never touches the EU is not covered.
Outermost regions such as Réunion, Martinique, the Canary Islands, Madeira and the Azores are part of the EU for this purpose.
Since Brexit, flights departing the United Kingdom fall under the UK's own retained version of the rules (often called UK261), not EU261, unless an EU carrier is flying into the EU.
Albania, Turkey, the UAE and the United States are outside the EU.
