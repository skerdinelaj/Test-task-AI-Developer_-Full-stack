# Internal claim-handling policy (example firm)

1. Every claim letter is reviewed and approved by a claims handler before it is sent. The agent never sends anything.
2. The letter must state the flight number, date, route, the disruption, the amount claimed and the legal basis (Regulation (EC) No 261/2004).
3. Claim only the amount produced by the rules engine. Never promise the customer a result.
4. Use a firm, polite and factual tone. Do not invent facts, dates or reasons that are not in the case file.
5. Ask the airline to pay within 14 days and to reply to the claims team, not to the customer directly.
6. If the airline cited extraordinary circumstances, or the facts disagree with the flight record, escalate to a senior handler.
7. Personal data (names, booking references, emails) must not appear in logs.
