# Denied boarding (Article 4)

If a passenger with a confirmed reservation who presented themselves on time is denied boarding against their will (for example because the flight was overbooked), the airline must pay compensation under Article 7, offer a choice between refund and re-routing, and provide care.
Denied boarding for reasonable grounds such as health, safety, security or inadequate travel documents does not give a right to compensation.
Passengers who volunteer to give up their seat in exchange for agreed benefits are not entitled to compensation for denied boarding.
